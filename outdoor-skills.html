Survival, Foraging & Knots

Offline-first outdoor reference guides, personal trail notes and a private foraging observation log. Live biodiversity lookups are optional and never used to declare a wild plant safe to eat.

Core guides work offline
Foraging safety: never eat a wild plant or fungus unless you are completely certain of its identity and suitability. A photo, app result or nearby observation is not enough. Check land access, local rules and protected species before collecting. For technical rope, climbing or rescue use, get qualified instruction and use appropriate certified equipment.
