Foraging Guide

Identify carefully, check dangerous lookalikes and use more than one trusted reference before collecting or consuming any wild plant.

If in doubt, do not eat it.Be 100% certain of identification, wash what you collect, avoid contaminated ground and busy roadsides, and never uproot a wild plant without permission.
