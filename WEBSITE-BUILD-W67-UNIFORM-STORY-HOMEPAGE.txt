Adventure Builder Website W67 — Uniform Story Homepage

Purpose
- Rebuild the homepage as one continuous story rather than a stack of boxes.
- Keep the side navigation as the main navigation, so the homepage does not need repeated feature buttons.

Homepage story
1. Every adventure starts with an idea.
2. Turn the idea into a plan.
3. Plan on the website and take the same journey onto the phone.
4. Experience the adventure outside.
5. Keep the route, journal, passport, photos and memories afterwards.
6. Close with: Plan it. Experience it. Remember it.

Design changes
- One light warm/off-white background through the complete story area.
- Removed alternating dark/light content blocks.
- Removed activity cards, memory boxes and repeated action buttons.
- Added a subtle route/story line and numbered chapters to connect the scroll.
- Laptop + phone artwork is frameless and blended into the page rather than placed inside a card.
- Only one main action button at the end of the story.
- Existing hero, side navigation, authentication, footer and website pages remain in place.
