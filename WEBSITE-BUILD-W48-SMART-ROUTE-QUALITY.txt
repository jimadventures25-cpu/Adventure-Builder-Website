Adventure Builder Website W48 — Smart Route Quality Engine

What changed
- Adds a separate trail-quality-engine.mjs module.
- Ranks generated loops using distance accuracy, repeated segments, sharp-turn density, self-crossing and loop closure.
- Uses at most three sequential Valhalla candidates and stops early on a strong match.
- Preserves the W46 protection: if routing falls through to the emergency app/GraphHopper gateway, no candidate burst is sent.
- Tightens initial loop radii to reduce severe overshoot.
- Jogging now penalises steps more strongly and favours continuous pedestrian surfaces.
- Cycling now avoids poor surfaces more strongly unless Adventurous mode is selected.
- Shows the selected route's quality score in the page status message.

Provider order and marker lifecycle from W46/W47 remain unchanged.
