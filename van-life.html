🌍 Live Tonight Search

Real mapped caravan sites, motorhome-tagged campsites and motorhome stopovers from OpenStreetMap.

Search an area or use your current location.

Data attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Location text search uses Nominatim only when you press Search; no autocomplete.

Permission status matters: a mapped campsite/caravan site or motorhome stopover is useful evidence, but rules can change. Check current signs, operator information, access, max-stay and local restrictions before staying. Ordinary car parks are excluded unless they are specifically tagged as a motorhome stopover.

Ordinary car parks are never automatically treated as legal or safe overnight stops. Future live results must carry a clear evidence/status label such as Official Site, Aire, Council Confirmed, Owner Verified, Community Reported or Unverified.
