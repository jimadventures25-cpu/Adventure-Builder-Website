Adventure Builder Website — Build W7 Photo Studio

Working beta tools:
- Local photo upload (JPG/PNG/WebP)
- Brightness, contrast, colour, warmth and soft blur
- Original, square, 16:9 and 9:16 centre crop
- Rotate and horizontal/vertical flip
- Coastal, sunset, forest, vintage and black-and-white looks
- Photo caption
- Adventure sticker
- Adventure Builder date stamp
- Hold-for-original comparison
- PNG export

Privacy:
