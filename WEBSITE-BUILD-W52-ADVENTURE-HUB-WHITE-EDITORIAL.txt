Adventure Builder Website W52 — Adventure Hub White Editorial Rebuild

Design direction
- White background throughout the Adventure Hub.
- Removes the dark dashboard/card visual language from W51.
- Uses spacing, typography and thin dividers to group content.
- Removes the redundant Quick Actions panel.
- Statistics are now open typography with separators, not a boxed dashboard card.
- Recent adventures and milestones are open list sections.
- Adventure Map becomes a full-width editorial feature and the real map is the dominant element on the Map tab.
- Feed cards lose outer frames and nested pills; content reads like an editorial gallery.
- Timeline rows, imports and forms use rules instead of panel boxes.
- Orange is reserved for active navigation, labels and emphasis.
- W50 data/import/map logic is retained unchanged.

Reference
Walking & Hiking W49 was used as the internal quality benchmark for its white canvas, restrained borders and spacing-led organisation.
