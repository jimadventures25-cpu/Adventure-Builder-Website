🏕️ Adventure Finder — Staged Rollout

Adventure Finder will grow from real discovery and direct booking links into verified owner listings and, later, optional booking inside Adventure Builder.

Stage 1 · Current🌍 Adventure Finder

Experience-first search, area filters and Adventure Match.

Stage 2🗺️ Real Adventure Database

Real campsites, POIs, trails, launch points and verified overnight-use evidence.

Stage 3🔗 Book Direct

Official website, phone and campsite booking links from each listing.

Stage 4🏕️ Owner Portal

Campsite owners claim and maintain their listing, photos and facilities.

Stage 5⭐ Verification

Mapped, Website Confirmed, Owner Verified and Adventure Builder Verified statuses.

Stage 6🧾 Book with Adventure Builder

Live availability and in-platform booking only where a campsite provides an approved integration.

Stage 7✨ Smart Adventure Planner

Build a complete trip from interests, time, travel radius and nearby experiences.

Stage 8🌐 Adventure Network

Community and partner discoveries connected across the UK and Ireland.

Stage 9🤝 Local Partners

Cafés, pubs, hire companies, attractions, outdoor shops and activity providers.
